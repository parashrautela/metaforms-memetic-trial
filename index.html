Metaforms

Honest and grounded AI infrastructure for research delivery teams

Client Metaforms ↗
Services Brand Identity, Illustration, Web Design & Development
Year 2025–2026

The Challenge

Metaforms sells AI infrastructure to market research agencies, a category full of cold gradients and generic robot imagery. They needed a brand that felt honest, human and grounded while still reading as serious software.

The Solution

A hand-drawn elephant mascot, warm cream and orange palette, serif headlines and sticky-note asides. We designed and built the full website around the story of removing one bottleneck at a time.

Month 1

Research + Mascot

Audited competitors, built a moodboard and explored illustration styles until the orange elephant emerged as the face of the brand.

Month 2-3

Website Design + Build

Wireframed the narrative, explored hero directions, then designed every section with custom illustration and diagrams of the delivery chain.

Month 3.5

Build + Launch

Built the responsive site with mobile-first recrops of every illustration and shipped it with the new footer wordmark and support pages.

Research + Competitor Analysis

We started by mapping the market research tooling landscape: how competitors position AI, how they talk to research delivery teams, and where the visual language of the category had gone stale. That audit set the direction for a warmer, more human brand.

competitor analysis board

The Elephant

Metaforms needed a character that felt honest and grounded, not another abstract AI gradient. We built a moodboard around elephants, explored dozens of illustration styles, and landed on a hand-drawn orange elephant that could live in real-world scenes across the site.

mascot inspiration moodboard
mascot illustration explorations
scene illustrations across the site

Wireframes

Low-fidelity wireframes for the homepage locked the narrative first: the problem, the delivery chain, the bottleneck, the proof. Every section had a job before it had a look.

From there we explored several hero directions, from dark and editorial to cream and illustrated, before settling on the "Honest and grounded" concept with the elephant reading under a tree.

homepage wireframes, full page layout
homepage hero explorations

The Website

A hand-drawn, warm, editorial site for a technical product. Serif headlines, orange accents, sticky-note asides and the elephant guiding readers from bottleneck to rollout.

Mobile

Every section was rebuilt for small screens rather than squeezed. Illustrations were recropped and the elephant reappears where the layout needed a beat.
